Notion AI vs ClickUp Brain (2026): Which One Is Worth Paying For?

Updated 22 October 2026 · 8 min read · AI for Work HQ Editorial

Key takeaways

  • Notion AI is bundled into the Business plan ($20/user/month, billed annually) — no separate AI bill.
  • ClickUp Brain is a $9/user/month add-on on top of any ClickUp plan ($7 Unlimited, $12 Business).
  • At 15 seats, Notion Business with AI costs $3,600/year; ClickUp Business + Brain costs $3,780/year.
  • Pick Notion if your work lives in documents and databases. Pick ClickUp if your work lives in tasks, sprints and projects.

Both tools now sell "AI that knows your work." The difference is where that work lives — and what the AI actually costs once you add it to a real team. That's what this comparison focuses on.

The short answer

Notion AI is the better value for knowledge-heavy teams (writers, agencies, ops, product) because you get AI without a second line item. ClickUp Brain is the better fit for delivery teams because its AI is wired into tasks, time tracking and project status rather than pages.

If you only need AI for writing and summarizing, neither is the cheapest option — a standalone assistant will do. These two are worth it when the AI can see your workspace.

What that means in practice

ClickUp looks cheaper on the sticker price ($7 vs $10 for the entry plans). The picture flips once you add Brain: Unlimited + Brain is $16/user, already above Notion Plus ($10) and approaching Notion Business ($20) — which includes AI.

Notion AI: what you actually get

Notion AI works inside your pages and databases. In day-to-day use that means:

  • Writing and editing in any page — drafts, tone changes, summaries, translations.
  • Q&A across your workspace — ask "what did we decide about pricing?" and get an answer with sources.
  • Database autofill — the AI fills properties like category, summary or next step for new entries.
  • Meeting notes and action items — useful if meetings are already documented in Notion.
  • Agents for recurring research and reporting tasks, billed separately in some setups.

Strengths: excellent for content, documentation, internal wikis and lightweight project tracking. The AI is where your knowledge already is, so it has context without integrations.

Weaknesses: it is not a project management tool at heart. Sprints, workload and dependencies require workarounds. Teams that live in Gantt charts will feel the limits.

ClickUp Brain: what you actually get

ClickUp Brain is built around tasks and projects:

  • Brain assistant and @Brain — ask questions about tasks, docs and status across the workspace.
  • AI writing and summaries inside tasks, docs and comments.
  • AI fields and cards — auto-fill custom fields, prioritize work, summarize progress.
  • AI Notetaker — meeting notes attached to the relevant project (hours-based add-on beyond the free allowance).
  • Model choice — you can route requests to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.

Strengths: the strongest option if your team runs on tasks, sprints and time tracking. AI that can see task status is genuinely useful for status updates and prioritization.

Weaknesses: the add-on model gets expensive at scale, and Brain's value collapses if your team doesn't actually maintain ClickUp properly. A messy workspace gives messy AI answers.

Which one should you pick?

Choose Notion AI if…

  • Your team writes, documents and plans in the same place.
  • You want one predictable price with AI included.
  • You're a solo operator, agency or content team of 1–15 people.

Choose ClickUp Brain if…

  • Your work is tracked as tasks with owners, due dates and statuses.
  • You need AI summaries of project status more than AI writing.
  • You'll pay for Business-tier features anyway and can absorb $9/user.

Skip both if…

  • You only need AI for writing — a standalone assistant is cheaper.
  • Your team won't maintain the workspace. AI on top of a messy workspace makes the mess faster.

FAQ

Is Notion AI included in the free plan?

No. Notion AI is included in the Business plan ($20/user/month, annual billing). Free and Plus users get a limited trial.

Is ClickUp Brain included in any plan?

No. Brain is a paid add-on on every plan: $9/user/month for Brain AI or $28/user/month for Everything AI (annual billing). The free plan includes a small trial allowance.

Can I use both?

Yes, and many teams do: Notion for knowledge and documentation, ClickUp for execution. Just be honest about the double cost — at 15 seats that's over $7,000/year combined.

Which is better for freelancers?

Usually Notion. At one seat, Notion Business with AI is $240/year; ClickUp Business + Brain is $252/year, and most freelancers need documents more than sprints.
